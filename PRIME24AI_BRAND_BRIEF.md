# Prime24AI Brand and Outreach Brief

## Brand
- Name: Prime24AI
- Positioning: Practical AI automation and supervised agent workflows for small and midsize businesses.
- Main site: https://prime24ai.com/
- Learn: https://learn.prime24ai.com/
- PitchMe: https://pitchme.prime24ai.com/
- Founder LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/darnley-weekes-60023b285
- Audience: U.S. businesses nationwide, especially professional services, staffing, property management, insurance, healthcare administration, accounting, and training providers.

## Voice
Conversational, direct, specific, calm, credible. No hype, no fake personalization, no “revolutionary,” no guaranteed results, and no generic AI language.

## Core message
Find one repetitive workflow, map the handoffs, add supervised assistance, keep human approval, and measure the outcome.

## Content goals
1. Teach businesses how to spot missed inquiries, slow follow-up, repetitive intake, and unclear handoffs.
2. Demonstrate one practical workflow at a time.
3. Invite public teardown requests and paid workflow audits.
4. Send interested people to the most relevant Prime24AI property.

## Campaign rules
- Draft-first. Never auto-publish or auto-message.
- Create platform-specific copy; do not cross-post identical text.
- Use only verifiable claims from the prospect's public website.
- Do not scrape private data, rotate accounts, or send mass unsolicited DMs.
- Include one clear CTA and one relevant URL.
- Use U.S. spelling and plain language.

## Today's campaign
Theme: The hidden cost of unclear intake and follow-up.
Offer: A short practical workflow review.
CTA: Ask for one process to review.
