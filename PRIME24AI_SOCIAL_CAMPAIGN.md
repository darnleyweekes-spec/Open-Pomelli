# Prime24AI Social Outreach Campaign

Use this campaign inside Open-Pomelli after loading:

https://prime24ai.com/

## Campaign goal

Generate qualified conversations with U.S. small and midsize businesses that have repetitive intake, slow follow-up, unclear handoffs, or customer-service bottlenecks.

## Offer

A short workflow review that identifies one process worth improving and gives practical recommendations. Keep the language grounded. Do not promise savings or results that have not been measured.

## Audience

Prioritize:
- Staffing and recruiting firms
- Property-management companies
- Accounting and bookkeeping firms
- Insurance agencies
- Healthcare administration and dental practices
- Training providers and professional associations
- Marketing, web, and IT agencies that could become referral partners

## Core message

Most businesses do not need another complicated software system. They need clearer intake, faster follow-up, fewer repeated questions, and better handoffs. Prime24AI builds focused, supervised workflows that keep people involved in approvals.

## Generate these drafts

### LinkedIn
Write a practical teardown post of one common workflow problem. Use a specific example, explain the human approval point, and invite one business owner to request a review. Include one relevant link.

### Facebook
Write a conversational local-business post that explains how missed inquiries and repetitive questions create avoidable work. Invite comments from business owners. Do not sound like an advertisement.

### Instagram
Create a five-slide carousel:
1. The customer inquiry arrives
2. The team has to sort it manually
3. Follow-up gets delayed
4. A supervised workflow creates a clear next step
5. Invite a workflow review

Provide short slide copy and a caption.

### X
Write three concise posts:
- A workflow observation
- A practical recommendation
- A low-pressure invitation to request a review

### TikTok / Reels
Write a 35–45 second script:
- Hook: “Your business may not need more software.”
- Show the repetitive workflow problem.
- Show the supervised handoff.
- State that a person remains in control.
- Close with the Prime24AI link.

## Writing rules

- Conversational and direct.
- No AI hype, fake personalization, or corporate filler.
- No “revolutionize,” “game changer,” “seamless,” or guaranteed ROI.
- Do not claim to have reviewed a specific company unless its public website was actually reviewed.
- Do not create automated DMs, mass connection requests, scraping, or fake engagement.
- Produce drafts only. A human must approve every post, comment, or message.
- Use different wording for every platform.
